Musical parts

Enter notes as NOTE:DURATION separated by spaces. Examples: C5:q D5:q E5:h F#5:8 G5:8. Use R for a rest: R:q. Durations: w = whole, h = half, q = quarter, 8 = eighth, 16 = sixteenth. Use # or b for accidentals. Octave numbers follow scientific pitch notation.

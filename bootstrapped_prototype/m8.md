===============================

No. You're right to question that. `f!'4` is not valid LilyPond syntax for cancelling an accidental.

In LilyPond, the exclamation mark is not the natural-accidental notation used in that example. I incorrectly carried over a syntax assumption.

The correct way to explicitly write a natural is:

lilypond

```lilypond
f!4
```

However, LilyPond's `!` is an explicit accidental reminder, not a natural sign. The pitch itself is determined by the note name and the active key signature.

The correct notation is simply `f'4`, without `!`. LilyPond automatically prints a natural sign when needed to cancel a previous sharp or flat.

### Expected behaviour

| Operation | LilyPond | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter F♯ | `fis'4` | F♯ |
| Lower semitone | `f'4` | F natural |
| Enter F again | `f'4` | F natural |
| Raise semitone | `fis'4` | F♯ |
| Insert barline, enter F | `f'4` | F natural |

The important distinction is that LilyPond notation stores the actual pitch, while LilyPond handles the printed natural sign automatically. Your JavaScript playback parser must separately remember the natural alteration until the barline.
